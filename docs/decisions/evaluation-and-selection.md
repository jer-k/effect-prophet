EP-085's logistic acceptance remains conditional on merging and independently verifying EP-094's fix; EP-086 metrics and EP-087 baselines are implemented locally; EP-088–090 and their tests/reviews remain pending. EP-039 was cancelled; the accepted [EP-092 caller-owned preparation decision](data-preparation-boundary.md) replaces it as the relevant input/schedule policy. EP-080's synthetic fitted-holdout evidence is descriptive, not calibration or an independent interpretation review; EP-081's benchmark evidence is descriptive, not a speed guarantee. ## Reference and ownership

Use unmodified Prophet 1.4.0 at [`abf69a215604afcaa7ecb4359f592d13bf6dea9f`, `python/prophet/diagnostics.py`](https://github.com/facebook/prophet/blob/abf69a215604afcaa7ecb4359f592d13bf6dea9f/python/prophet/diagnostics.py), especially `generate_cutoffs` (47–90), `cross_validation` (93–257), `single_cutoff_forecast` (260–321), `prophet_copy` (324–393) and `performance_metrics`/`rolling_*_by_h` (398–596). Its mutable fitted model retains history and fit controls; our [portable fitted state](../usage/model-persistence.md) deliberately does not. Therefore callers provide **complete observed history and fit options** and an existing `FittingBackend` Layer; no hidden history, fit-control recovery from a fitted model, new backend registry, calendar/frequency inference or synchronous-WASM parallelism. Results are immutable/caller-owned evaluation values, not fitted handles. Public operations retain Effect's typed failures and normal completion semantics.

## Plan, units and deterministic budgets

The EP-083 encoded input is `{ horizonMs, cutoffs: { mode: "explicit", timestamps } | { mode: "generated", initialMs?, periodMs? } }`. Timestamps use the existing canonical UTC codec; durations are **positive safe integer milliseconds, at most 315,360,000,000 (3,650 × 86,400,000 ms)**. Computed endpoints must be representable by the timestamp codec and safe integer arithmetic; fail, never wrap or clamp. `initialMs` and `periodMs`, when supplied, use the same domain; `initialMs` may exceed the data span and then yields a plan error. Fractional milliseconds and calendar months are not supported. `horizonMs` for a result row is its _elapsed_ `timestamp − cutoff`, not the configured maximum horizon. Preserve caller-owned schedules from EP-092.

Parse all history rows with `decodeObservations`, then full fit-option syntax with `decodeOptions`, then plan syntax; generated-default derivation follows syntax. Before planning, check original explicit changepoints against the full observed history's **inclusive** timestamp range using the existing `fit` semantic rule, without fitting or loading WASM: invalid original points must not become valid merely by being filtered out of every fold. Explicit cutoffs do not resolve seasonalities. Empty/duplicate/unordered canonical explicit cutoff lists fail (not sort/deduplicate). Each cutoff must be strictly later than the first history instant and `<= lastHistory − horizonMs`, and must have at least one assessment row. Training is all observations `timestamp <= cutoff`; assessment is `cutoff < timestamp <= cutoff + horizonMs`. A cutoff need not be an observation. The successful fold contains indexes only into the current validated history; output exposes a cutoff/count summary, not array aliases or portable fold indexes. No universal two-row fit minimum: the selected Layer decides it in its fold fit. Invalid explicit folds fail as a _whole plan_, not silently disappear.

Generated cutoffs follow release `generate_cutoffs`: begin at `lastHistory − horizon`; walk backward by period while the current last cutoff is `>= firstHistory + initial`; after decrement, if `(cutoff, cutoff+horizon]` has no observations and cutoff remains above the first history timestamp, replace it by `latest observed timestamp <= cutoff − horizon`; append, finally discard the last attempted cutoff and reverse. Check each arithmetic operation and final fold. If no legal fold remains, report a plan error. This preserves Prophet's backwards step and irregular-gap adjustment; do not substitute an evenly spaced grid or drop an empty generated fold. Default `periodMs = ceil(horizonMs/2)` (Python's half duration has finer-than-ms precision when the horizon is odd). Default `initialMs = max(3*horizonMs, longest *statically requested* seasonality in milliseconds)`: convert each positive finite `periodDays` with checked `ceil(periodDays * 86_400_000)` (the shortest integral-ms window not less than the configured period), with checked multiplication and the same duration/endpoint bounds (a derived default beyond the limit fails; never silently shortens the initial window). Explicit custom and explicitly enabled built-in periods count; `"auto"` built-ins count their defined periods (yearly 365.25 days, weekly 7 days, daily 1 day) **conservatively even if a fold will disable them**. `"off"` does not count. This static, history-independent upper bound deliberately differs from Prophet's full-history resolved seasonalities: future data cannot turn on an auto definition in the planner. An explicit `initialMs` is never raised to the seasonality period; unlike Prophet's warning, users choose it knowingly, and fold-local resolution remains train-only.

Preflight budgets: at most **10,000 history rows, 128 folds and 1,000,000 total assessment forecast instances** per plan. Before any fit, calculate bounds with checked integer multiplication/addition. For direct interval cross-validation, at most **8,000,000 aggregate assessment-row × sample cells**, with Stage G's **10,000 rows and 1,000,000 row × sample cells per fold** checked before the first fit. For search, at most **32 candidates, 2,048 candidate-fold executions, 2,000,000 aggregate training-row visits, 1,000,000 aggregate assessment-row visits and 8,000,000 aggregate assessment-row × sample cells**; these count the declared full plan even if a candidate later fails. Point mode counts zero sample cells. The underlying per-call uncertainty limits (including 1,000,000 row × sample cells) still apply. Holdout allows at most **10,000 development rows, 10,000 holdout rows and 16 baseline definitions**; the same per-call uncertainty bounds apply. Pure metric input is limited to **1,000,000 validated forecast instances** and a rolling target count no larger than that input. These are safety/work caps, not timeout or performance guarantees. No implicit history copying in planning; execution may allocate owned fold slices. Resource failures are typed before execution and must not truncate the plan.

## Fold-local computation and precedence

For each ascending fold, take only the training prefix and call ordinary public `fit` with original fully specified options except for the **fold-local explicit changepoint projection**. Prophet's `prophet_copy` uses `< last_history_date` (its comment about `<= cutoff` is misleading). Keep only explicit changepoints **strictly before the fold's final training observation**; points at or after that observation are discarded. A point exactly at the final observed training timestamp is discarded even if the cutoff is later. A point at the first training timestamp is allowed by existing inclusive `fit`; this differs from Python's dummy-point representation when the retained set is empty. Empty retained sets are valid; do not invent a changepoint. Validate original options syntax and full-history explicit-point bounds before projection, and never filter automatic candidates: ordinary fold-local `fit` resolves them. Do not fit the full history merely to validate options or pre-resolve auto state.

Each assessment prediction row copies timestamp, regressors, conditions, capacity and floor and **omits only target `value`**. Keep target separately by original assessment index; never join on timestamp or allow target into fit, scaling, normalization, feature resolution, changepoint placement, initialization or simulation. Static event/regressor/conditional definitions are reused. Prediction and `predictUncertainty` consume complete known future inputs, not future targets. Check forecast/interval counts, order, timestamps, finiteness and bounds before attaching actuals. Same observed timestamp across folds is a distinct forecast instance identified by fold and cutoff. A fold that fails returns no partial successful result and prevents later folds from running.

The point result is `{ kind: "point", plan, rows, folds }`; EP-084 publishes point rows `{ fold, cutoff, timestamp, horizonMs, actual, predicted }`. EP-085 atomically adds `{ kind: "intervals", simulation, sampleCount, intervalWidth, plan, rows, folds }` with each interval row containing all point fields plus finite ordered `lower`/`upper` (value interval; never optional one-sided bounds). Fold summaries contain index, cutoff, trainingCount, assessmentCount and model kind, not fitted models. Results copy/freeze owned records; sample buffers and raw backend frames do not escape. Do not publish a union member until its producer exists.

| Simultaneous conditions                                                          | Winner / boundary                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bad full-history row/empty history and bad options/plan                          | Existing indexed `InputValidationError(input="observations")`.                                                       |
| Valid history, bad option syntax/full-history explicit-point bounds and bad plan | `InputValidationError(input="options")`; reuse `fit`'s existing bounds rule without fitting.                         |
| Bad plan syntax and impossible model-specific configuration                      | `InputValidationError(input="evaluation-plan")`; no fold/backend.                                                    |
| Valid syntax, no legal folds/overflow/work excess and unsupported Layer          | Whole-plan `InputValidationError(input="evaluation-plan")` before any fold.                                          |
| Valid plan, insufficient fold history/unsupported fit                            | Fold `EvaluationError(stage="fit")` wrapping original `FittingError`/`UnsupportedConfigurationError`; no later fold. |
| Fit succeeds, assessment lacks aligned future values, loader broken              | Fold `EvaluationError(stage="predict")` with indexed `InputValidationError` before prediction WASM load.             |
| Point prediction fails and uncertainty would also fail                           | Point `predict` cause wins; do not simulate.                                                                         |
| Forecast/interval frame count/order/timestamp/bound mismatch                     | Fold `EvaluationError(stage="result")` or `EvaluationError(stage="interval-result")`; no completed result.           |

For interval mode, parse uncertainty options **after plan syntax but before plan semantics/resource checks**, including a required uint32 root seed, `samples` 1..2048, width `(0,1)` and intervals-only output; reuse Stage G defaults and schema rather than a second parser. Complete resource preflight precedes fitting. If model/Layer support for uncertainty is knowable without fitting, fail preflight before fold 0; otherwise wrap the exact `PredictionError` in `stage="uncertainty"` after fitting, with no fabricated bands. The complete fold sequence is fit → ordinary point predict → `predictUncertainty(output="intervals")` → alignment. Simulation failure stops the fold; result width and sample count are metadata, not quality claims.

Errors: extend `InputValidationError.input` with `"evaluation-plan"`, `"evaluation-metrics"`, `"evaluation-baseline"`, `"evaluation-search"` and `"evaluation-holdout"` as their tickets land. `EvaluationError` is a tagged context union: fold failures have bounded `fold` index, integer `cutoff`, `stage: "fit" | "predict" | "result" | "uncertainty" | "interval-result" | "baseline"`, stable reason and optional row index; separate `stage: "holdout"` failures have `step: "fit" | "predict" | "uncertainty" | "result" | "report"` and optional row index, **no fictitious fold/cutoff**. Retain the original typed `InputValidationError | UnsupportedConfigurationError | FittingError | PredictionError` (or precisely typed result mismatch) as a **non-enumerable in-memory cause**, not a flattened string. `EvaluationMetricError` carries metric/aggregation/bucket and `"unavailable" | "zero-actual" | "empty-bucket" | "non-finite" | "misaligned"`; `EvaluationSearchError` carries candidate index/ID and `"candidate-failed" | "no-success"`, with original in-memory cause when applicable. Allowed fold `(stage,reason)` pairs are `("fit","fit-failed")`, `("predict","prediction-failed")`, `("result","result-mismatch")`, `("uncertainty","uncertainty-failed")`, `("interval-result","interval-result-mismatch")`, `("baseline","baseline-unavailable" | "non-finite")`. Holdout pairs are `(step,"operation-failed")` for fit/predict/uncertainty and `(step,"result-mismatch")` for result/report. The typed original cause lives only in memory. Portable failure projections contain only bounded tag/reason/stage/fold/candidate-index/row-index information and applicable underlying typed _reason_; omit messages, external causes and stacks. Raw timestamps/candidate IDs appear only in explicit caller results/errors, never spans; report storage remains caller-controlled. Defects are not converted into recordable failures.

## Seed protocol (EP-085/088)

Use a **versioned evaluation seed schedule `effect-prophet-eval-seed-v1`**, separate from the simulation identity (`prophet-map-scalar-xoshiro128ss-v2` after EP-094). Start 32-bit FNV-1a at offset basis `2166136261`, processing each byte as `hash = imul(hash XOR byte, 16777619) >>> 0`. Byte stream, in order: UTF-8 schedule label and NUL; uint32 little-endian root seed; length-prefixed UTF-8 simulation identity (uint32 little-endian byte length); signed 64-bit little-endian horizon; uint32 little-endian cutoff count; all validated ascending cutoffs as signed 64-bit little-endian integers; length-prefixed UTF-8 candidate identity; signed 64-bit little-endian current cutoff. `candidate identity` is empty for direct cross-validation and the parsed caller ID for search, so there is no ambiguous concatenation. All epoch/duration inputs are checked safe integers before BigInt byte encoding. The resulting uint32 is the fold's simulator seed. Do not include fold execution order, observations, targets, metric choice, tracing, formatting, other candidate options or ambient RNG. Same plan/root/ID/cutoff/simulation version replays in the same build; adding/removing a plan cutoff intentionally changes the plan identity. Golden vectors (both cutoffs `2024-01-10T00:00:00.000Z`, `2024-01-17T00:00:00.000Z`; horizon `604800000`; simulation identity v2): root `0`, empty candidate ID, first cutoff → `3165536520`; root `19`, candidate ID `cp-0.10`, second cutoff → `4063750176`. EP-085 tests these exact vectors; EP-088 must reuse this primitive, not copy it. A schedule change gets a new label; simulator changes retain their own separate version policy.

## Metrics (EP-086)

Publish `mae`, `mse`, `rmse`, `mape`, `mdape`, `smape` and `coverage`. Explicit, nonempty unique metric selection; never silently remove MAPE or coverage. Define `e=actual−predicted`. MAE `mean(|e|)` in target units; MSE `mean(e²)` in squared units; RMSE `sqrt(mean(e²))` in target units **after** bucket aggregation. MAPE `mean(|e/actual|)` and MDAPE `median(|e/actual|)` are **ratios**, not values multiplied by 100; median of an even count averages its two central sorted values using checked finite arithmetic. `actual===0` (including `-0`) follows a required `mapeZeroActual: "error" | "exclude"` policy shared by MAPE and MDAPE; default **`"error"`**. Under `"exclude"`, count included/excluded rows per bucket and fail if none remain. Near-zero nonzero values are not silently excluded; nonfinite divisions fail. sMAPE `mean(2|e|/(|actual|+|predicted|))` as a ratio, with exact `actual=predicted=0` contribution zero. Coverage is the fraction satisfying **`lower <= actual <= upper`**. Point results requesting coverage fail with typed `"unavailable"`. All nonfinite arithmetic and aggregation fail, not return NaN/Infinity.

Modes: `rows` preserves fold/input order (count 1); `horizons` groups exact positive integer elapsed milliseconds ascending; `overall` includes every forecast instance; `rolling` right-aligns on each distinct horizon after ascending sort, including all instances at that horizon and whole preceding horizon groups until at least `w` rows. No equal-horizon group is split; actual bucket `rowCount` may exceed `w`. All metrics share the same raw bucket; MAPE/MDAPE under explicit exclusion use and report their smaller included denominator. `w` is a positive integer count or `max(1, floor(fraction * totalRows))` for finite `0 < fraction <= 1`. Only emit horizons with at least `w` cumulative rows; no empty buckets. This whole-group policy is a bounded difference from Prophet's weighted boundary for means and different median window selection; it avoids fractional row counts and ensures the raw bucket identity is stable across reported metrics (except explicitly excluded percentage-zero rows). Counts are row/forecast-instance counts, not independent series counts; overlapping folds deliberately repeat observations. Pure metric loops add no spans or WASM work. Rows provide the custom-analysis seam; no global Python metric registry.

## Baselines and comparison (EP-087)

Baseline definitions: `last-observation`, `training-mean`, `seasonal-naive(lagMs)`; lag uses the positive bounded duration codec. For each fold, use only its training targets/timestamps. Last observation repeats the last training value; mean uses checked/compensated finite summation; seasonal-naive predicts assessment instant `t` only from an **exact** training instant `t−lagMs` (checked subtraction). If any instant lacks that training match, fail the whole baseline operation with bounded fold/row context; do not interpolate, infer frequency, fill or silently compare unequal row sets. One-row training supports last/mean. Baselines do not inspect features or run fit/WASM. Comparison requires identical `(fold,cutoff,timestamp,horizonMs,actual)` instances and metric/policy definitions; report side-by-side values only, **no implicit skill denominator or statistical significance**. Baseline unavailability is not converted into a model failure.

## Search, final holdout and portable reports (EP-088/089)

Search accepts 1..32 explicit ordered candidates, each with a **complete** encoded fit-options value and a unique ASCII ID matching `[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._-]{0,63}`. Preparse every candidate, shared history, plan and metric policy and check aggregate budgets before candidate 0. One supplied Layer serves all candidates; execute candidates and their folds sequentially. Objective is one **overall** finite point-error metric from MAE/MSE/RMSE/MAPE/MDAPE/sMAPE, always minimized (no hidden maximize/coverage scoring); its zero-actual policy is explicit. Interval cross-validation may be requested, but ranking uses its point forecast and one root seed with candidate-isolated fold seeds. `failurePolicy: "record" | "fail-fast"`: record only expected candidate-local typed failures as safe portable outcomes and continue; fail-fast carries candidate context and original typed cause; all failed yields `EvaluationSearchError(reason="no-success")` plus candidate outcomes. Stable ties choose lowest _original candidate index_; candidate IDs do not affect ranking except isolated interval seeds. Successful candidate results retain score and metric report; full CV rows are **opt-in** and otherwise omitted to limit retained memory. Never share fold fits between candidates or claim the selected configuration is a fitted handle. Search cannot access final holdout data.

`evaluateHoldout` accepts a trusted selected result/receipt from search, the complete selected options, nonempty strictly ordered development and nonempty strictly ordered holdout histories with `firstHoldout > lastDevelopment`, the same metric policy used for development selection, optional baseline definitions and optional interval controls with a separate explicit root seed. Verify receipt/index/ID/options identity against the supplied search result before fit; this is consistency checking, **not cryptographic provenance**. No holdout target is supplied to selection or development fit. Fit once on _all_ development rows, point-predict holdout prediction projections, optionally interval-simulate, compute identical-row baseline/metric summaries, and construct a JSON-compatible version-1 report. Same train-only projection, failure order and support policy as folds; do not rank again or refit with holdout. A holdout-target change may change only actuals, metrics and coverage, not selection, fitted state, forecasts or seeds. A caller repeatedly inspecting holdouts is outside what types can prove.

Report includes policy/seed-schedule/simulation versions, declared plan and metric policy, development/holdout counts and range, selected ID/options/score, candidate outcomes, baseline definitions/outcomes, finite forecast instances and metrics, model kind and safe environment/build provenance supplied by the caller. Full options, identifiers, observations and forecasts may be sensitive: report is **caller-controlled data**, not telemetry. No fitted handles, full training rows, backend frames, RNG state, Effect causes/stacks, secrets or arbitrary metadata. Use strict schema-backed encode/decode, checked counts/order/alignment/finite fields, reject excess/missing/inconsistent data; decoding does not refit. Report format is experimental/unversioned across package releases despite its local schema version; no promise of cross-version migration. File/database I/O is application-owned. Holdout preflight includes row/options/receipt/partition/budget checks before fit; unexpected fit/predict/interval failures retain typed stage context, and report construction is all-or-nothing.

## Observability, evidence and ticket reconciliation

Public `Effect.fn` operations: `Prophet.crossValidate`, `Prophet.searchModels`, `Prophet.evaluateHoldout`; a public planner/parser or baseline may be named only when actually exposed. For each executed fold one `effect-prophet.evaluation.fold` span encloses projection, fit, point predict, optional simulation, verification and failure translation; one `effect-prophet.evaluation.candidate` span encloses each executed candidate, and one `effect-prophet.evaluation.holdout` encloses the whole holdout execution. Existing public fit/predict/uncertainty and coarse WASM spans descend naturally. Attributes: only bounded operation/model/growth, fold/candidate index/count, training/assessment/sample counts and uncertainty-enabled flag. No timestamps/cutoffs, user IDs/names, options, values, scores, seeds, messages, causes or raw protocol data. A recorded typed candidate failure ends its candidate span failed even if search later succeeds; unexpected defects retain ordinary Effect semantics. Syntax, planning, support preflight and pure metrics/baselines must not emit false integration spans. Test with an in-memory `Tracer.make`, real Layers and checked direct parent IDs, trace IDs, exits and containment; no tracer/exporter requirement or sleeps.
