# MAP uncertainty: statistical validation protocol

**Status:** EP-080 validation in progress. The design below is fixed before calculating fitted-holdout results. No nominal coverage claim is approved.

## Evidence classes

1. **Known-state process.** The committed [fixed-state Prophet 1.4.0 scalar fixtures](../../integration/fixtures/prophet-1.4.0/map-uncertainty.json) use `sample_posterior_predictive(vectorized=False)`, not Prophet's default grid approximation. Real generated WASM compares population means, variances and equal-tailed 10th/90th percentiles of fixed linear, flat, implicit-floor logistic and explicit-floor mixed/crossing-rate logistic states. The latter uses 8,192 draws per language; four independent WASM requests of 2,048 draws are pooled only in the test. Integer RNG/replay, shared-path continuation, bounded logistic trends, flat/no-point and analytically derived compound-Poisson variance are separately checked by native Rust and generated WASM. NumPy and xoshiro draws are not pairwise equal. The exact zero-rate forced case is a continuous-logit **extension**, not Python release parity.
2. **Fitted synthetic holdouts.** Fit each training prefix through the real public MAP Layer and assess only later complete rows. The fixed recipe below deliberately separates held-out observations (which include irreducible noise and possible misspecification) from fixed known future covariates and bounds. This is not a test of Prophet's optimizer identity and does not establish universal calibration.

The public known-state sanity check fits a noisy flat MAP history, saves and reloads it, then conditions on its **stored** fixed trend and output-unit `noiseScale`. With two future rows, seed 19, 2,048 draws and width 0.8, it checks a constant trend, independent row observation noise, sample value mean within `0.08*sigma`, population variance within `0.15*sigma²`, and empirical interval endpoints within `0.15*sigma` of `trend ± 1.281551565545*sigma` (the analytic Gaussian 10th/90th percentiles). These are Monte Carlo tolerances, not fitted-objective or holdout coverage tolerances.

## Predeclared fitted-holdout design

Use four independent series per scenario, with series seeds `101, 211, 307, 401`; no failed fit is replaced. Generate per-series Gaussian observation errors using a locally seeded test-data PRNG, independent across series and between train and holdout; the forecasting API's explicit simulation seed is `9000 + seriesSeed`. The test-data generator is not part of the library simulator. Fit to the first 21 daily UTC rows and assess the next two days at exact daily instants, using 512 simulated draws, width 0.8, and the same rows/seed after JSON encode/decode. Scores are computed on _interval value_ bounds, not on bounded logistic trend bands. No trained coefficients, scales, or feature resolutions are computed from the holdout.

Preselected cases, in output units:

| Case           | Data-generating train/holdout mean                                                       | Fitted model                                                                                                                     | Known future inputs                                                             |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| stable         | `4 + 0.15*d + 0.3*sin(2*pi*d/7)` with Gaussian sd `0.2`                                  | linear MAP with one explicit day-10 changepoint and weekly Fourier order 1                                                       | timestamps                                                                      |
| changing       | `4 + 0.15*d + 0.25*max(0,d-10)` with Gaussian sd `0.2`                                   | linear MAP with one explicit day-10 changepoint                                                                                  | timestamps                                                                      |
| flat           | `4 + 0.3*sin(2*pi*d/7)` with Gaussian sd `0.2`                                           | flat MAP with weekly Fourier order 1                                                                                             | timestamps                                                                      |
| logistic-mixed | `-2 + 102*sigmoid(6*(d/20-0.45)) + 1.5*(d%2)` with Gaussian sd `0.2`                     | minmax logistic MAP, implicit floor from training, one known additive binary promotion regressor and explicit day-10 changepoint | row capacity `100`, promotion parity; implicit floor is persisted, not supplied |
| shock          | same stable generator on training; held-out mean additionally shifts by `+3` (unmodeled) | same linear MAP as stable                                                                                                        | timestamps, **no** shock indicator                                              |

The logistic generator is intentionally imperfect: its output offset and additive promotion can put a noisy observation outside a trend bound. A logistic fit failure counts as failure, not as a reason to drop that series. Series seeds are reused **across scenarios** for comparable noise streams, not treated as independent cross-scenario comparisons. Within each scenario the four series use independent streams; the two forecast rows share a latent simulated path and must not be counted as independent Bernoulli trials.

Record per case: number of attempted fits (4), succeeded/failed fits and stable typed failure reasons; for successful fits, the per-series fraction of the two heldout values in inclusive `[lower,upper]`, per-series average width and their arithmetic mean. Give the sample standard deviation of replicate-level coverage and width divided by `sqrt(successCount)` where at least two fits succeed; otherwise mark the standard error unavailable. This standard error describes series-to-series variation, not a population coverage guarantee. Do not assert coverage equals 0.8, rank models, or omit the shock scenario. One pass over five cases produces 20 fits and at most 20 * 512 * 2 simulated cells; keep it offline, synchronous and sequential. No multiple-comparison significance claims are made; bounds are process checks, not 5 independent hypothesis tests.

## Observed results

Computed offline through `test/uncertainty.test.ts` with the predeclared recipe; all 20 fits completed. Coverage is the mean of four **series-level** fractions (each fraction is based on two future rows). SE is the sample standard deviation across four series divided by two. Values below are descriptive, not thresholds or a claim that the 80% nominal width is calibrated.

| Scenario       | Fit succeeded / attempted | Mean coverage ± replicate SE | Mean value-band width ± replicate SE |
| -------------- | ------------------------: | ---------------------------: | -----------------------------------: |
| stable         |                     4 / 4 |                0.375 ± 0.125 |                      0.5083 ± 0.0188 |
| changing       |                     4 / 4 |                0.375 ± 0.125 |                      0.5587 ± 0.0106 |
| flat           |                     4 / 4 |               0.500 ± 0.2041 |                      0.5221 ± 0.0318 |
| logistic-mixed |                     4 / 4 |                1.000 ± 0.000 |                      3.6851 ± 0.0731 |
| shock          |                     4 / 4 |                0.000 ± 0.000 |                      0.5083 ± 0.0188 |

No series were excluded; no fit failures occurred for these particular seeded inputs. Zero replicate SE for logistic/shock reflects identical _binary coverage fractions in four cases_, not certainty about population coverage. Linear stable/changing cover substantially fewer observations than the nominal width in this small experiment; the deliberate held-out shock has no coverage at all while retaining the same width as stable. Do not infer a universal coverage rate from four series or repair this finding by increasing the sampler width after observing it.

## Interpretation and review

These intervals are marginal, equal-tailed empirical quantiles of shared latent paths plus independent Gaussian observation noise, **conditional on the fitted MAP coefficients and provided future data**. They are not simultaneous trajectory bands, coefficient confidence intervals or calibrated guarantees. Flat/no-point MAP histories have fixed conditional trends; extrapolation continues the historical changepoint-rate assumption. Seasonal/event/regressor coefficients, future covariates/capacities and unpredictable shocks are not sampled. OLS residual bands, Bayesian parameter uncertainty, automatic anomaly decisions and imputation are excluded. Changing width or sample count controls quantiles and Monte Carlo noise; it cannot cure model misspecification. A small sample count makes tail estimates unstable. Same-build replay requires the complete ordered rows/model/options/algorithm and seed, including after JSON reload. Synchronous WASM blocks its calling thread; an Effect timeout cannot preempt it.
